PAID MEDIA / PERFORMANCE CTR 3.2 → 5.1% TEST  /  LEARN  /  REFINE 01 / PAID MEDIACAREER EVIDENCE ↗ Tuning paid campaigns
for better efficiency Search, audience and creative optimisation across Google and Meta Ads. VIEW CASE NOTES +

Marketing focus

Google Ads, Meta Ads, paid campaign optimisation and qualified lead generation.

Business situation or challenge

A business wants more qualified enquiries from paid advertising while keeping its monthly budget under control. A representative plan splits $1,200 monthly between Google Ads ($800) and Meta Ads ($400).

My understanding of the problem

Clicks alone cannot explain performance. The key is to find where irrelevant traffic, weak messaging or a mismatched landing page prevents interested prospects from becoming qualified leads.

My approach

Review objectives, audience and keyword targeting, ad messaging and landing-page experience. Compare CTR, CPC, conversion rates and lead quality to identify friction. Campaign outcomes included 8–10 qualified leads (around 16 raw leads) monthly, Google Ads CTR rising from 3.2% to 5.1%, wasted clicks falling 15%, and a CPL comparison improving from $130 to $105 (20% lower).

Practical recommendations

  • Review search terms and exclude irrelevant traffic.
  • Refine keywords and audience targeting.
  • Test headlines, messaging and creative.
  • Align landing pages and calls to action with the ad promise.
  • Verify conversion tracking before budget decisions.
  • Shift spend toward campaigns producing better-qualified leads.

Key learnings

Judge campaigns by qualified leads, conversion rates and business outcomes—not traffic volume alone. CPC and conversion performance can differ by channel, so evaluate each against lead quality.

ORGANIC / SEARCH VISIBILITY +35%TRAFFIC SIGNALS COMPOUND 02 / SEOCAREER EVIDENCE ↗ Growing organic
search visibility Technical, on-page and local search improvements backed by analytics. VIEW CASE NOTES +

Marketing focus

SEO, keyword research, technical SEO, content optimisation and organic search visibility.

Business situation or challenge

A website offers useful services and content but attracts limited relevant organic traffic and visibility for its target search terms.

My understanding of the problem

Search performance depends on more than keywords: pages need to match search intent, be easy to crawl and index, and connect users to useful information.

My approach

Start with keyword, search-intent and competitor research. Assess technical health, page structure, content relevance and internal links; then prioritise improvements. Organic visits grew from 400 to 540 per month (+35%), impressions increased 20% over six months, and 12 target keywords reached Google's top 10.

Practical recommendations

  • Choose keywords that match audience needs and search intent.
  • Improve page titles, meta descriptions and headings.
  • Check indexing, crawlability and site structure.
  • Strengthen internal links and organise content clearly.
  • Use competitor research to find content gaps.
  • Monitor impressions, clicks and keyword visibility in Google Search Console.

Key learnings

Sustainable SEO combines technical health, relevant content and ongoing optimisation. Rankings matter, but attracting the right audience and improving useful visits are the real goals.

Marketing focus

AI-assisted research, campaign planning, content ideation and marketing operations.

Business situation or challenge

Marketing teams spend significant time researching audiences, developing campaign ideas, preparing content and handling repetitive tasks across multiple channels.

My understanding of the problem

AI is most useful when it supports a real campaign need—improving preparation and options while leaving strategic decisions, accuracy and quality control with the marketer.

My approach

Use AI-assisted tools to generate and refine campaign ideas, support audience and competitor research, develop initial content concepts, assist planning and explore workflow efficiencies. Apply this alongside SEO, paid media, email and CRM work.

Practical recommendations

  • Start with a clear campaign brief, audience and objective.
  • Use AI to explore ideas, research questions and message variations.
  • Check outputs against trusted information and brand context.
  • For a HubSpot workflow, use AI to sketch logic, review conditions and surface potential edge cases.
  • Validate the workflow against actual lifecycle rules, then test it in HubSpot before activation.
  • Automate repetitive tasks only when the process and quality checks are clear.

Key learnings

AI can improve the speed of marketing work, but good execution still depends on human judgement, accurate information and quality checks. The goal is better marketing—not simply more content.
